My Communities

Building communities around gaming, developing my own game called "Beach Simulator" and running my own clothing store have been some of the most rewarding parts of my Roblox journey. They gave me the chance to connect with a wide range of people, each bringing their own culture, taste, and perspective. What started as simply interacting with players online grew into something bigger: an appreciation for the sheer depth of history and experience that exists in the world beyond the screen. These three communities capture what I've built both for the Roblox community and for myself.
