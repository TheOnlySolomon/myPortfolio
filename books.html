Favorite Reads (2024 – 2026)

The list of books I found most impactful throughout my two years of reading. Each book has been exciting and intellectually stimulating, offering new perspectives and ideas that have greatly enriched my curiosity and growth as an inquisitive thinker.

5.0 / 5.0 Politics
Lee Kuan Yew: The Man And His Ideas

Han Fook Kwang / Warren Fernandez / Sumiko Tan

An insightful account of Lee Kuan Yew's life, political ideas, and the transformation of Singapore.

5.0 / 5.0 Self-help
The Psychology Of Money

Morgan Housel

A compelling exploration of how emotions, behavior, and personal experiences shape the way we think about and manage money.

5.0 / 5.0 History
A Brief History Of Singapore And Malaysia

Christopher Hale

A concise exploration of the history, politics, and development of Singapore and Malaysia, from their colonial past to the formation of the modern nations.

5.0 / 5.0 Psychology
The Psychology Book

Catherine Collin et al.

An engaging introduction to psychology, exploring 100 influential theories, experiments, and ideas from some of the field's most important thinkers.
